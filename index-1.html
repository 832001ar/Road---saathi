ℹ️ About Road Saathi

Travel with confidence. Get roadside help when you need it.

1. What is Road Saathi?

Road Saathi is a roadside assistance platform that connects customers with nearby service providers when vehicle help is needed.

2. Our Purpose

Our purpose is to make it easier to find trusted roadside help and connect customers with available service providers.

3. Services

  • Bike Repair
  • Car Repair
  • Tow Truck
  • Fuel Delivery
  • Tyre Repair
  • Tourist Assistance
  • Emergency Driver

4. For Customers

Select a service, provide your vehicle and location details, find nearby providers, contact a provider and confirm the service with them.

5. For Service Providers

Service providers can register, select their services, set their service location and radius, stay Available / ON and receive nearby service requests.

6. Road Saathi's Role

Road Saathi provides the connection platform. The actual service, price and deal are between the customer and service provider.

7. Safety & Emergency

Emergency numbers are available for safety support. In a serious emergency, official emergency services should be given priority.

8. Privacy

Information and location may be used according to the app's service, matching and support requirements. See Terms & Privacy for more details.

9. Support

For service, account, payment or app problems, use the Help & Support section.

10. App Information

📄 Terms & Privacy

Please read these Terms & Privacy carefully before using Road Saathi. This page explains how the app may be used and how information may be handled.

1. Purpose of Road Saathi

Road Saathi is designed to connect customers with roadside service providers such as mechanics, tow trucks, fuel delivery, tyre repair, tourist assistance and emergency drivers.

2. User Responsibility

  • Provide accurate name and mobile information during registration.
  • Do not misuse another person's identity or information.
  • Provide accurate vehicle and location details when requesting a service.
  • Do not use the app for fraud, illegal activity or to harm anyone.

3. Service Provider Responsibility

Service providers should keep their profile, service, experience, location and charges accurate. The actual service/deal between customer and provider is the responsibility of both parties. Road Saathi provides the connection platform.

⚠️ Service Charge Notice:
Service provider ko customer se kaam hone ke baad working charge batate samay applicable Road Saathi service charge include karke final amount batana hoga.

4. Location & GPS

Location/GPS information may be needed to match a service request with nearby providers. Location sharing may be used according to the service requirement. Users can control location permission from their device.

5. Mobile & OTP

Mobile number and OTP may be used for account verification and login. Do not share your OTP with anyone. Users are responsible for keeping their account secure.

6. Service Charges & Payment

Applicable Road Saathi service charges or provider charges may be shown according to the service. Check the amount before making a payment. Keep payment confirmation and transaction details safely.

7. Emergency Services

Police, Ambulance, Fire Brigade and All Emergency numbers are provided for safety support. In a serious emergency, give priority to official emergency services. Road Saathi is not a replacement for emergency services.

8. Privacy & Information

Information provided in Road Saathi may be used for account management, service matching, requests, communication, support and app functionality. The aim is to avoid unnecessary public display of personal information. Users should keep their personal information secure.

9. Account Deletion

Users can delete their Road Saathi profile using Delete My Profile in Settings. Some information may need to be retained for legal, security or transaction-record purposes.

10. Changes to These Terms

Road Saathi may update app features, services or these Terms & Privacy in the future. Important changes may be shown in the app.

11. Support

For service, account, payment or privacy problems, use the Help & Support section of the app.
